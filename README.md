# Tradingview-strategy
Extract a list of trade data in CSV format

Step-by-Step Instructions:

1. Scroll back up to the very top of your List of Trades tab.
2. Open your browser console, paste the code below, and press Enter.
3. You will see a message: "Scraper armed! Now slowly scroll down your list..."
4. Manually scroll down to the bottom of the list using your mouse or trackpad. Take your time so TradingView has a moment to load the numbers.
5. Once you hit the bottom (Trade #1), go back to your console and type downloadTrades(), then press Enter.
